The Problem

Streaks guilt you. Leaderboards stress you out.

Most apps motivate with numbers that reset your anxiety every morning. We think you'll show up for something you actually care about — like a pet who's counting on you.

😰 Other apps

  • 🔥 "Don't break your 47-day streak!" (pure guilt)
  • 🏆 Leaderboards that make beginners feel behind
  • 🪙 Pets/skins bought with coins — cosmetic only
  • 📉 XP that means nothing once the app closes

🐾 PawLingo

  • ❤️ Your pet's energy dips when you're away — it misses you, not a number
  • 📊 A private progress dashboard, not a public ranking
  • 🌱 Evolution tied to real Listening/Speaking/Reading/Writing skill
  • 🎩 Coins still exist — but only for hats and outfits, never growth
